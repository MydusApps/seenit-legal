# Seenit Support

Need help with Seenit? Email us at [help@mydus.co.uk](mailto:help@mydus.co.uk) and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I add a friend?
Open the **Find Friends** screen from your profile. You can search by username or invite people from your contacts. Send a follow request and once they accept, their logs will appear in your feed.

### How do I log a film or TV show?
Tap the **Log** button, search for the title, and tap to confirm. Your friends will see it in their feed.

### Can I edit or delete a log?
Yes. Open your profile, find the log you want to change, and use the menu to edit the date or delete it.

### How do I delete my account?
Open **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This permanently removes your profile, logs, and follow relationships. The action cannot be undone.

### How do I report a bug or inappropriate behaviour?
Email [help@mydus.co.uk](mailto:help@mydus.co.uk) with as much detail as you can — what you were doing, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. For inappropriate behaviour, include the username and a brief description of the issue.

### How do I turn off notifications?
Open **Settings → Notifications** in the app, or change the permission in your device's system settings.
